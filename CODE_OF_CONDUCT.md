# Code of Conduct

OpenSend follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive,
and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include demonstrating empathy,
respecting differing viewpoints, giving and accepting constructive feedback, taking responsibility
for mistakes, and focusing on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, sexualized language or attention, trolling, insulting
or derogatory comments, publishing another person's private information, and other conduct that
would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies in project spaces and when representing the project in public. Maintainers are
responsible for clarifying and enforcing it and may remove, edit or reject contributions and
interactions that violate it.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through an available private GitHub
channel. Until a dedicated confidential conduct address is published, do not put sensitive
personal information in a public issue. Maintainers will respect the reporter's privacy and apply
responses proportionate to the circumstances.

Enforcement may range from a private correction, through a temporary restriction, to a permanent
ban for sustained or severe violations. This document is adapted from the Contributor Covenant
2.1; its enforcement guidance is available on the linked site.
